Before you power on

  1. Unplug everything while you move wires.
  2. Rail A + and Rail B + never touch. Only the two − rails are joined, by the GND jumper in column 60.
  3. 12V stays off the breadboard. Barrel + → relay COM1, relay NO1 → solenoid, solenoid → barrel −.
  4. The diode's stripe goes on the wire coming from NO1.
  5. Check the cut USB cable reads +5V on red with a multimeter before the servo goes on.
  6. Each LED's long leg sits in the resistor's column (E26 green, E34 red). The short leg goes one hole to the right.
  7. A 30-pin DevKit with pins in rows B and I leaves exactly one free hole per pin: row A on top, row J below. A wider 38-pin board only leaves one side free. Use two breadboards or F-M jumpers for that.
  8. Many breadboards split each rail in the middle. If yours has a break in the red/blue line, bridge it with a short jumper.
  9. Pin order on your board and modules may differ from this drawing. Match the printed labels, not the positions.
  10. Optional: a 470–1000µF capacitor across Rail A + and −, near the servo, striped leg to −.
