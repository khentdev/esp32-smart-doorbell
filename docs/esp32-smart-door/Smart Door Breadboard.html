Before you power on

  1. Unplug everything while you move wires.
  2. Rail A + and Rail B + never touch. Only the two − rails are joined, by the GND jumper on the right edge.
  3. 12V stays off the breadboard. Barrel + → relay COM1, relay NO1 → solenoid, solenoid → barrel −.
  4. The diode's stripe goes on the wire coming from NO1.
  5. Check the cut USB cable reads +5V on red with a multimeter before the servo goes on.
  6. Each LED goes through a 220Ω resistor; the long leg faces the resistor.
  7. Pin order on your board and modules may differ from this drawing. Match the printed labels, not the positions.
  8. Optional: a 470–1000µF capacitor across Rail A + and −, near the servo, striped leg to −.
