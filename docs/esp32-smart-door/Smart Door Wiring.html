Smart Door Wiring

Every part of the smart door wired point to point, with no breadboard. Lever connectors (or terminal strips) take the place of the power rails: one for logic 5V, one for servo 5V, and two ground blocks bridged together. Pins match firmware/include/pins.h and the Hardware Setup Guide. Click a part, a colour below, or a table row to highlight just those wires.

Before you power on

  1. Unplug everything while you wire.
  2. Never join the two 5V blocks. Logic 5V comes from the ESP32's VIN, servo 5V from the charger. Only the grounds are shared, through the link wire between the two GND blocks.
  3. 12V stays away from the ESP32. Barrel + → relay COM1, relay NO1 → solenoid, solenoid → barrel −.
  4. The diode's stripe goes on the wire coming from NO1.
  5. Check the cut USB cable reads +5V on red with a multimeter before the servo goes on.
  6. Solder each 220Ω resistor to the LED's long leg, then cover the joint with heat-shrink. The short leg goes to the GND block.
  7. Insulate every joint and every unused wire end (fingerprint Touch / 3.3VT, cut USB wires).
  8. Put servo and charger wires in a lever connector or screw terminal. Twisted bare wire works loose under the servo's current.
  9. Pin order on your board and modules may differ from this drawing. Match the printed labels, not the positions.
  10. Optional: a 470–1000µF capacitor between the servo 5V and servo GND blocks, striped leg to GND.
